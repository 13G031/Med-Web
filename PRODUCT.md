## Users

- Hospital clinicians, emergency departments, and surgical departments evaluating hemostatic and absorbent products.
- Medical-device procurement, tender, and distribution teams verifying registration, specifications, and purchasing history.
- Emergency-response, public-safety, and institutional buyers evaluating field-use readiness.
- Researchers and partners looking for patents, technical mechanisms, clinical evidence, and product documentation.

## Product Purpose

Present Yangling Andiyuan Biotechnology Co., Ltd. and its two core medical-device products with enough technical, regulatory, and clinical detail for professional evaluation and inquiry. Success means a visitor can quickly understand the products, verify key credentials, locate evidence, and contact the company.

## Positioning

The company combines a Class III drug-device reviewed emergency hemostatic dressing with a patented Class II PVF medical sponge, covering active hemostasis, absorption, liquid management, support, and wound-care applications.

## Operating Context

The products are evaluated and used across pre-hospital emergency care, hospital emergency departments, surgery, puncture-site hemostasis, wound care, cavity packing, fluid absorption, public-safety supply, and centralized medical procurement.

## Product Principles

- Make professional evidence easy to find, not buried in promotional copy.
